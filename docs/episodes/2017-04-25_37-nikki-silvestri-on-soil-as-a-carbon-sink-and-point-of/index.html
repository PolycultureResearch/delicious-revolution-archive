Nikki Silvestri

On soil as a carbon sink and point of engagement for new alliances

April 25, 201734 min

Download MP3

Nikki Silvestri is an advocate for climate solutions, heathy food systems, and social change. As the Co-Founder of Live Real and former Executive Director of People's Grocery and Green for All, Nikki has built and strengthened social equity for underrepresented populations in food systems, social services, public health, climate solutions, and economic development. Her many honors include being named one of The Root's 100 Most Influential African Americans in 2014. Nikki is Co-Founder and CEO of Soil and Shadow, a project design and management firm working to support thriving communities, economies, and natural environments. In this episode, Nikki talks with Devon about the ways sequestering carbon in soil present opportunities to forge new alliances, and the need to address our shadow sides to make those alliances work.
